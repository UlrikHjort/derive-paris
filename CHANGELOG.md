# Changes

## 0.5.0 (October 2026): the first public version

The city:
- **Paris from OpenStreetMap:** inside the Périphérique, with the Bois de
  Boulogne, Vincennes and a corner of La Défense. Buildings have their
  real outlines, heights and roofs. The streets have markings and traffic
  lights, and the Seine has its quays and bridges. There are trees and
  street lamps, and the ground has the real heights from IGN.
- **The landmarks** are built in detail: the Eiffel Tower, the Arc de
  Triomphe, Notre-Dame, the Pont Alexandre III, the Opéra, the Hôtel de
  Ville, the Statues of Liberty, and the Zouave of the Pont de l'Alma.
- **Day and night:** the real sun and moon for the date, rain, storms,
  snow, mist on the Seine, the winter flood, spring blossom, summer heat
  and autumn leaves.
- **Life:** traffic that follows the hours of the day, and pedestrians
  with dogs and baguettes. There are markets, Vélib' stations, café
  terraces, delivery vans, street sweepers, pigeons, roadworks,
  accidents and demonstrations. Bateaux-mouches and péniches run on the
  Seine, and snowploughs come out in the snow.
- **The calendar's days:** the 14th of July with its parade and
  fireworks, Christmas, New Year, and other surprises.

Getting around:
- **Driving** with a manual or automatic gearbox, or a Logitech G29 wheel.
  Real speed limits, a GPS with address search, and a minimap.
- **Flying** a light plane: under the bridges, and through the Arc de
  Triomphe.
- **On foot:** up the Eiffel Tower and the Arc, aboard a bateau-mouche,
  down into the metro, and into a few places worth finding.
- **On a Vélib'**, or in a motorboat of your own on the Seine.
- **Paris in 1973** (U): the cars of the time, and the city before the
  Pyramid, the Centre Pompidou and the Opéra Bastille.

Games (F2):
- **Street racing:** ready-made tracks, random races, or tracks you
  make yourself.
- **Taxi:** fares all over Paris, the meter and tips, police with speed
  guns, "follow that car!", tourists who want a sight on the way, and
  the rush on a rainy night.
- **Time trials** through Paris.
- **The morning round:** bread on a scooter at dawn.
- **The night bus:** a line of real stops after midnight.

And more:
- **Postcards** of the famous places (F4), photos (F12) and videos (F9).
- **A car radio** (Z) with the news on the hour.

To try it: the quick start in the README (`make`, `make tiles-small`,
`make run`).
